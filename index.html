Why Neko Health?

I actually signed up on your scan waiting list years ago—so I need absolutely no convincing about the profound necessity and immediate commercial benefits of proactive, preventative health. I have lived and breathed this philosophy from afar as an operator, and up close as a consumer tracking your journey from Sweden to London.

Speaking candidly: the medical marketing agency I founded made good money, but over time, it lacked true meaning. Our tools were optimized to help individual practices scale, but it often felt like we were just making rich doctors richer.

I want to dedicate the next decade of my career to something systemic, data-driven, and genuinely transformative. Neko’s strong sense of mission—shifting humanity away from treating illness when it arrives to sustaining health before it's ever at risk—is something I will throw myself into with raw passion, commercial execution, and unmatched alignment.
